# Essential Documents & Land Records Guide for Farmers and Cooperative Members

## Master Document Checklist (PACS Loan, KCC, PMFBY, Govt Schemes)
Keep both physical photocopies and digital copies (e.g., DigiLocker) of these core documents:
1. **Identity & Address Proof:** Aadhaar Card (linked with an active mobile number for OTP/e-KYC).
2. **Land Ownership Records:**
   - **7/12 Extract (Satbara Utara / सातबारा उतारा):** In Maharashtra, the primary land record showing village name, survey/gat number, total area, owner/occupant names (Kabjedar), crop cultivation details, and existing loans/encumbrances (Boja).
   - **8A Extract (आठ-अ उतारा):** Consolidated holding sheet listing all land parcels owned by a farmer in a village along with total land area and land revenue assessment.
   - *(In other Indian states: Khasra, Khatauni, Jamabandi, or Patta/ROR).*
3. **Bank Account Details:** Front page of Bank Passbook or cancelled cheque clearly showing Account Holder Name, Account Number, and IFSC code. Ensure **Aadhaar Seeding / NPCI DBT Mapping** is active so scheme subsidies and insurance claims don't fail.
4. **Sowing Certificate / Crop Declaration (Pik Pera Dakhla / बुवाई प्रमाण पत्र):** Certifies which crop is sown on which survey number during the current Kharif/Rabi season. Farmers in Maharashtra can self-register their crop via the **E-Pik Pahani (ई-पीक पाहणी)** mobile app, or obtain certification from the village Talathi/Patwari.
5. **Photographs:** 2 to 4 recent passport-size photographs.
6. **Tenant / Sharecropper Proof (if applicable):** Registered lease agreement or landowner consent affidavit for non-owning cultivators applying for PMFBY or tenant loans.

## How to Download Digital 7/12 and 8A Extracts Online (Maharashtra)
1. **Free Viewing (Informational Copy):**
   - Visit `https://bhulekh.mahabhumi.gov.in` (Mahabhulekh).
   - Select your Division (Vibhag), District, Taluka, and Village.
   - Search by Survey/Gat Number or Name to view/print an unverified copy.
2. **Digitally Signed Legal Copy (Valid for Banks, PACS, Loans & Courts):**
   - Visit `https://digitalsatbara.mahabhumi.gov.in`.
   - Login using your mobile number and OTP (or Aaple Sarkar credentials).
   - Select District, Taluka, Village, and Survey/Gat Number.
   - Pay the nominal government fee (usually ₹15 per extract) via UPI/Net Banking.
   - Download the **QR-coded, Digitally Signed 7/12 or 8A PDF**, which requires no physical stamp from the Talathi.

## How to Check Aadhaar-Bank DBT Seeding Status
Many PM-KISAN and PMFBY payouts fail only because Aadhaar is not mapped to the bank account on NPCI:
1. Visit `https://myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in` -> "Bank Seeding Status" (requires Aadhaar + OTP).
2. Or visit your bank branch/PACS and submit an **Aadhaar Seeding / DBT Mandate Form**.

## Removing Old Loan Encumbrance (Boja / बोजा कमी करणे) from 7/12
After fully repaying a PACS or bank crop loan:
1. Obtain a **No Dues Certificate (NDC / बेबाकी दाखला)** and Loan Clearance Letter from the PACS/Bank.
2. Submit the NDC to the Talathi office or online via `https://pdeigr.maharashtra.gov.in` / e-Ferfar portal so the encumbrance entry (Mutation / Ferfar) is cleared from your 7/12 extract.
