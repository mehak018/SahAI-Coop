# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) – Complete Farmer Guide

## Overview and Objectives
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is the Government of India's flagship crop insurance scheme protecting farmers against financial loss from unforeseen crop damage or failure across the entire crop cycle—from pre-sowing to post-harvest.

## Eligibility
- All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas are eligible, including sharecroppers and tenant farmers.
- Enrollment is **voluntary** for all farmers (both loanee farmers taking KCC/crop loans and non-loanee farmers).
- Loanee farmers who do not wish to enroll can opt out by submitting a written declaration to their bank/PACS at least 7 days before the cut-off date.

## Farmer Premium Rates (Maximum Cap)
Farmers pay a very low, subsidized share of the actuarial premium, while the remaining premium is subsidized equally by the Central and State Governments:
- **Kharif Season (All food grains & oilseeds):** Maximum **2.0%** of Sum Insured (or actuarial rate, whichever is lower).
- **Rabi Season (All food grains & oilseeds):** Maximum **1.5%** of Sum Insured.
- **Annual Commercial & Horticultural Crops:** Maximum **5.0%** of Sum Insured.
- **Maharashtra State Note:** Under Maharashtra's Sarvasamaveshak Pik Vima Yojana (when notified by the State Government), farmers have been able to enroll by paying a nominal ₹1 contribution with the state bearing the remaining farmer share—farmers should check the current season's Maharashtra Agriculture Department notification at `pmfby.gov.in` or their local CSC/PACS.

## How to Apply / Enroll
Farmers can register before the notified seasonal cut-off date through any of these channels:
1. **Online Portal:** National Crop Insurance Portal at `https://pmfby.gov.in` ("Farmer Corner" -> Apply for Crop Insurance).
2. **Bank / PACS:** Visit your Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS), District Central Cooperative Bank (DCCB), or commercial bank branch.
3. **Common Service Centre (CSC) / Aaple Sarkar Seva Kendra:** Walk in with required documents for assisted enrollment.
4. **Mobile App:** Official "Crop Insurance" mobile app (available on Google Play Store).

## Documents Needed for PMFBY Enrollment
- Aadhaar Card (mandatory for authentication and Direct Benefit Transfer).
- Land ownership proof: 7/12 extract (Satbara Utara) and 8A extract, or Khasra/Khatauni; tenant/sharecropper lease agreement where applicable.
- Bank passbook copy with active account number and IFSC code (Aadhaar-seeded for DBT).
- Sowing Certificate / Crop Declaration (Pik Pera Dakhla) or self-declaration of sown crop.
- Active mobile number for SMS alerts.

## Risks Covered Under PMFBY
1. **Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk:** Severe deficit rainfall or adverse weather preventing sowing (up to 25% of sum insured).
2. **Standing Crop (Sowing to Harvesting):** Non-preventable risks such as drought, dry spells, floods, inundation, widespread pest/disease attacks, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm, and cyclone.
3. **Post-Harvest Losses:** Coverage up to **14 days** after harvesting for crops kept in "cut and spread" or small bundled condition in the field against hailstorm, cyclone, cyclonic rain, and unseasonal rain.
4. **Localized Calamities:** Hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst, and natural fire affecting isolated farms.
5. **Add-on Coverage:** Crop loss due to attack by wild animals (where notified by the State).

## How to Report Crop Loss & File a Claim (72-Hour Rule)
For **localized calamities** or **post-harvest losses**, farmers MUST intimate the loss within **72 hours** of the event:
1. **Krishi Rakshak Portal & Helpline:** Call toll-free national helpline **14447** (available in Hindi, Marathi, English, and regional languages).
2. **Crop Insurance Mobile App:** Submit geotagged photos and loss details directly.
3. **Online:** Report via `https://pmfby.gov.in`.
4. **Offline:** Submit written intimation to your bank/PACS, local Agriculture Officer (Krishi Sahayak / Taluka Agriculture Officer), or the insurance company's district office.

## Claim Settlement Process
- For widespread area-based losses, claims are calculated automatically based on Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) and YES-TECH/satellite yield estimates—no separate claim form is needed.
- For localized/post-harvest losses, a joint loss assessment is conducted by the insurance surveyor and agriculture official.
- Approved claim amounts are credited directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
- Farmers can track application and claim status using their Application/Receipt Number on `pmfby.gov.in` or by calling **14447**.
