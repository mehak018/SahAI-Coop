# Primary Agricultural Credit Societies (PACS) & Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

## What is a PACS?
A **Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS)** (known in Marathi as *Vividh Karyakari Seva Sahakari Sanstha* / *Prathmik Krushi Patsanstha*) is the foundational village-level cooperative institution in India's three-tier short-term cooperative credit structure (PACS at village level -> District Central Cooperative Bank [DCCB] at district level -> State Cooperative Bank [StCB] at state level).

## Core Services Offered by PACS
1. **Short-Term & Medium-Term Agricultural Credit:** Provides seasonal crop loans for Kharif and Rabi crops through Kisan Credit Card (KCC), as well as medium-term loans for farm machinery, drip irrigation, dairy, and allied activities.
2. **Agricultural Inputs at Fair Prices:** Supplies certified quality seeds, subsidized fertilizers (urea, DAP, NPK), pesticides, and agricultural implements at the village level.
3. **Common Service Centre (CSC) Services:** Over 40,000+ computerized PACS function as CSCs offering 300+ digital e-services including banking, bill payments, Aadhaar updates, PMFBY enrollment, PM-KISAN registration, and land record downloads.
4. **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samriddhi Kendras (PMKSK):** One-stop shops at PACS for fertilizers, soil testing information, seeds, and agri-advisory.
5. **Grain Storage & Marketing:** Under the World's Largest Grain Storage Plan in the Cooperative Sector, PACS are building decentralized godowns, custom hiring centers, and procurement centers so farmers avoid distress selling after harvest.
6. **Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Kendras:** Eligible PACS also operate Jan Aushadhi pharmacies providing affordable generic medicines to rural families.
7. **Micro-ATM & Banking Correspondence:** Farmers can deposit/withdraw cash and check balances locally through PACS linked with DCCBs.

## How to Become a Member of a PACS
1. Visit your village PACS office (secretary/manager) and request the **Membership Application Form**.
2. Eligibility: You must be an adult (18+ years), residing or owning/cultivating agricultural land within the PACS area of operation.
3. Submit required documents: Aadhaar Card, 7/12 & 8A land extracts, passport-size photos, and bank passbook copy.
4. Pay the nominal entrance fee and minimum share capital prescribed in the society's bylaws (usually ₹10 to ₹100 per share).
5. The Managing Committee reviews and approves membership (under cooperative laws, an application must generally be decided within a stipulated period, typically 2–3 months, or the applicant can appeal to the Registrar).

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC) & Interest Subvention
- **Purpose:** Timely, affordable working capital for crop cultivation, post-harvest expenses, animal husbandry, dairy, and fisheries.
- **Interest Rate:** Standard short-term crop loans up to **₹3 lakh** carry a concessional interest rate of **7% per annum** (supported by Government of India's Modified Interest Subvention Scheme).
- **Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI):** Farmers who repay their KCC crop loan on time receive an additional **3% interest subvention**, bringing the effective interest rate down to **4% per annum**.
- **State Zero-Interest Schemes (e.g., Maharashtra Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Interest Rebate Scheme):** Eligible farmers repaying crop loans up to ₹3 lakh within the due date often receive an additional state interest rebate, making the effective interest rate **0%** (subject to prevailing state government GRs).
- **Collateral-Free Limit:** Crop loans up to **₹1.60 lakh** (recently revised upward to **₹2 lakh** under RBI guidelines) do not require collateral security.
- **Validity:** KCC is typically valid for 5 years with annual renewal and flexible withdrawal/repayment.
