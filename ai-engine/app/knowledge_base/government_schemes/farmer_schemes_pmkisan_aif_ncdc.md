# Key Government Schemes & Subsidies for Farmers and Cooperatives

## 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit:** Direct income support of **₹6,000 per year** to eligible landholding farmer families, paid in **three equal installments of ₹2,000** every four months directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts (DBT).
- **Eligibility:** All landholding farmer families with cultivable land as per state land records (excluding institutional landholders, income tax payers, serving/retired high-grade government employees, and constitutional post holders).
- **Mandatory Compliance:** Farmers must complete **e-KYC** (via OTP on `https://pmkisan.gov.in`, Facial Authentication on the PM-KISAN app, or Biometric e-KYC at a CSC) and ensure Land Seeding + Aadhaar-Bank Seeding are marked "Yes".
- **Check Status:** Visit `https://pmkisan.gov.in` -> "Know Your Status" or call PM-KISAN Helpline **155261 / 011-24300606**.
- **Maharashtra Bonus – Namo Shetkari Mahasanman Nidhi Yojana:** Eligible Maharashtra farmers registered under PM-KISAN receive an **additional ₹6,000 per year** from the Maharashtra State Government (totaling ₹12,000/year combined).

## 2. Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF)
- **Portal:** `https://agriinfra.dac.gov.in`
- **Purpose:** Medium-to-long-term financing for post-harvest management infrastructure and community farming assets—such as warehouses, cold storages, grading/sorting units, ripening chambers, primary processing units, and custom hiring centers.
- **Eligible Beneficiaries:** Farmers, PACS, Marketing Cooperative Societies, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), Self Help Groups (SHGs), Joint Liability Groups (JLGs), and agri-entrepreneurs.
- **Key Financial Benefits:**
  - **3% Interest Subvention** per annum on loans up to **₹2 crore** for a maximum period of **7 years** (lending rate capped around 9%, making effective interest ~6% or lower).
  - **Credit Guarantee Coverage** under CGTMSE for loans up to ₹2 crore (guarantee fee borne by the Government of India).
  - Can be converged with other Central/State capital subsidy schemes (like PMFME, MIDH).

## 3. NCDC (National Cooperative Development Corporation) Schemes
- **Portal:** `https://www.ncdc.in`
- **Role:** Provides financial assistance (term loans, working capital, and margin money/subsidies) to cooperative societies across India.
- **Major Cooperative Schemes:**
  - **Yuva Sahakar:** Encourages youth-led cooperative startups with subsidized interest rates.
  - **Ayushman Sahakar:** Assists cooperatives in establishing rural hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities.
  - **Nandini Sahakar:** Women-focused framework supporting women cooperatives in dairy, food processing, handicrafts, and agri-business with interest subvention and capacity building.
  - **Dairy, Fisheries & Sugar Cooperative Support:** Funds milk chilling units, cattle feed plants, cold chains, and cooperative sugar mill modernization/ethanol plants.
  - **Sahakar Mitra (SIP):** Paid internship scheme for young professionals in cooperatives.

## 4. PM-KUSUM (Solar Pumps & Clean Energy for Farmers)
- **Benefit:** Provides high capital subsidy (typically **60% combined Central & State subsidy**, plus 30% bank loan—farmer pays only ~10% upfront) for installing standalone off-grid solar agriculture water pumps (Component B) and solarizing existing grid-connected pumps (Component C).
- **Maharashtra Note:** Farmers in Maharashtra can also apply under **Magel Tyala Saur Krushi Pump Yojana** via the Mahadiscom portal (`https://www.mahadiscom.in/solar`).

## 5. Soil Health Card & e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)
- **Soil Health Card (`https://soilhealth.dac.gov.in`):** Free soil testing every 2 years with crop-wise fertilizer recommendations to reduce chemical fertilizer costs and improve yield.
- **e-NAM (`https://www.enam.gov.in`):** Pan-India electronic trading portal networking APMC mandis so farmers and FPOs/PACS can sell produce transparently through online competitive bidding with direct online payment.
