# Cooperative Grievance Redressal & Dispute Resolution Guide

## Common Issues Members Can Report
Farmers and cooperative society members have a legal right to raise formal complaints regarding:
- Unjustified denial of cooperative society membership or crop loans.
- Corruption, financial misappropriation, or fake loan entries in a member's name.
- Refusal by the Managing Committee or Secretary to show audit reports, bylaws, or account statements.
- Non-conduct of Annual General Meetings (AGM) or election irregularities.
- Delayed or wrongful rejection of PMFBY crop insurance claims.
- Non-refund of matured deposits from a cooperative credit society (Patsanstha).

## Step-by-Step Escalation Path for Cooperative Complaints

### Level 1: Written Representation to the Society
- Submit a written complaint/application addressed to the **Chairman / Secretary** of the cooperative society or PACS.
- Always keep a **signed and stamped acknowledgement copy** (date of receipt) as proof.

### Level 2: Registrar of Cooperative Societies (Most Effective Statutory Remedy)
If the society does not resolve the issue within 15–30 days (or if the complaint is against the committee itself):
- **Taluka Level:** Assistant Registrar of Cooperative Societies (ARCS / सहाय्यक निबंधक, सहकारी संस्था).
- **District Level:** District Deputy Registrar of Cooperative Societies (DDR / जिल्हा उपनिबंधक, सहकारी संस्था).
- **State Level:** Commissioner for Cooperation and Registrar of Cooperative Societies (Maharashtra: Sahakar Ayuktalaya, Pune – `https://sahakarayukta.maharashtra.gov.in`).
- **Powers of the Registrar:** Under Sections 83 and 88 of the Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, the Registrar can order an official **Inquiry**, **Inspection**, special **Audit**, or **Surcharge recovery** against erring committee members/secretaries, and can even supersede a defaulting Managing Committee.

### Level 3: Cooperative Court (Section 91 Disputes)
- Disputes touching the constitution, elections, management, or business of a cooperative society (such as disputed loan recovery, member rights, or society vs. member financial disputes) can be filed before the **Co-operative Court** (e.g., under Section 91 of the MCS Act, 1960).
- Appeals against Co-operative Court orders go to the **State Co-operative Appellate Court**.

### Level 4: Online Government Grievance Portals
1. **CPGRAMS (Centralized Public Grievance Redress and Monitoring System):**
   - Portal: `https://pgportal.gov.in`
   - Select Ministry of Cooperation (for Multi-State Cooperatives / NCDC / national schemes) or Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (for PMFBY / PM-KISAN).
   - You receive a unique Registration Number to track action taken online.
2. **Maharashtra Aaple Sarkar Grievance Portal:**
   - Portal: `https://grievances.maharashtra.gov.in`
   - File complaints directly to the Co-operation, Marketing & Textiles Department or Revenue Department.
3. **Multi-State Cooperative Societies (MSCS) Ombudsman:**
   - For societies operating across multiple states (registered under MSCS Act 2002, amended 2023), members can complain to the **Co-operative Ombudsman** appointed by the Central Government (`https://mscs.dac.gov.in`).
4. **PMFBY Crop Insurance Grievances:**
   - Call **Krishi Rakshak Helpline 14447**, lodge a grievance on `https://pmfby.gov.in`, or escalate to the District Level Grievance Redressal Committee (DGRC) chaired by the District Collector.

## Essential Evidence to Attach With Any Complaint
- Copy of your society membership passbook/share certificate and Aadhaar.
- Copies of loan statements, payment receipts, or 7/12 extract showing disputed entries.
- Copy of prior written complaint submitted to the society (with date/acknowledgement).
- Clear chronology of dates, amounts, and names of involved officials.
