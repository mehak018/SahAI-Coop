# Cooperative Society Rules, Governance & Legal Member Rights

## Foundational Legal Framework
- **State Cooperative Societies:** Governed by the respective State Co-operative Societies Act (in Maharashtra, the **Maharashtra Co-operative Societies [MCS] Act, 1960** and MCS Rules, 1961).
- **Multi-State Cooperative Societies:** Societies operating in more than one state are governed by the **Multi-State Co-operative Societies (MSCS) Act, 2002** as amended in **2023** (which introduced the Co-operative Election Authority, Co-operative Ombudsman, and stronger governance rules).
- **Constitutional Protection:** The 97th Constitutional Amendment recognized the right to form cooperative societies under Article 19(1)(c) and promoted democratic, autonomous, and professional functioning of cooperatives.

## Fundamental Rights of Every Cooperative Society Member
1. **Democratic Voting Right ("One Member, One Vote"):**
   - Regardless of how many shares a member holds, every active member has **exactly ONE vote** in general meetings and elections. Proxy voting is generally prohibited for individual members.
2. **Right to Participate in Annual General Meeting (AGM):**
   - Every society must convene an AGM at least **once every financial year** (typically within 6 months of the close of the financial year, i.e., by 30th September).
   - Members have the right to receive prior written notice (usually 14 clear days), attend, ask questions about finances, approve budgets, and vote on resolutions.
   - Members can also demand a **Special General Body Meeting (SGBM)** if a prescribed requisition (typically 1/5th of total members) signs a written request.
3. **Right to Inspect Records & Obtain Copies (Transparency):**
   - Under Section 32 of the MCS Act (and equivalent provisions across states), every member has the statutory right to inspect—free of charge during office hours—the society's **Bylaws (Upavidhi)**, **Register of Members**, **Annual Balance Sheet**, **Profit & Loss Account**, **Audit Report**, and **Minutes of General Body Meetings**.
   - Members can obtain certified copies of these documents upon paying the nominal prescribed copying fee (typically within 30 days of application).
4. **Right to Fair Share of Surplus / Dividend & Patronage Rebate:**
   - After statutory reserve fund allocations (usually at least 25% of net profits to the Reserve Fund), net profits may be distributed to members as dividends (subject to statutory caps, e.g., up to 15% under MCS Act) or patronage bonuses.
5. **Right to Contest Elections:**
   - Any eligible active member who is not a loan defaulter or otherwise disqualified under the bylaws/Act has the right to contest elections for the Managing Committee / Board of Directors.
6. **Right to Nominate a Legal Heir:**
   - Every member can nominate a person (Form of Nomination) to whom their shares and interest in the society will be transferred upon the member's death.

## Governance, Elections & Mandatory Audits
- **Board / Managing Committee Term:** Typically **5 years**, with elections conducted by an independent **State Co-operative Election Authority (SCEA)** (or Central Co-operative Election Authority for multi-state societies).
- **Mandatory Reservation on Boards:** Seats are reserved for Scheduled Castes/Scheduled Tribes, Women (typically 2 seats), and Other Backward Classes / weaker sections as mandated by law.
- **Mandatory Annual Audit:** Every cooperative society's accounts MUST be audited at least once every financial year by a certified auditor from the government panel. The Audit Report and Audit Rectification Report (Form 'O') must be placed before the General Body in the AGM.
- **Disqualification of Committee Members:** A committee member can be disqualified for defaulting on society loans, missing consecutive board meetings without leave (usually 3 meetings), committing fraud, or failing to convene the AGM or conduct mandatory audits on time.

## Active Member Duties
- Attend General Body Meetings regularly (under MCS Act, remaining absent for consecutive AGMs or not availing minimum services may classify a member as a "non-active member", which can restrict voting rights until reactivated).
- Repay cooperative loans on schedule to keep the society financially healthy and eligible for interest rebates.
- Abide by the registered Bylaws of the society.
